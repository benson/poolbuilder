how is this pool generated?

each set uses mtgjson's weighted booster configs: pack variants, sheets, and card weights are sampled directly from the published model. scryfall is only used afterward for card images.

why does the card count vary by set?

standard play boosters are 13 playable cards = 78 in a 6-pack sealed pool. some sets add a 14th slot:

  • modern horizons 3 — new-to-modern reprint slot
  • outlaws of thunder junction — big score bonus sheet
  • strixhaven — mystical archive
  • tarkir: mutant turtles — guaranteed legendary turtle
  • edge of eternities — stellar sights

older sets used 15-card draft boosters (90 per sealed pool).

is this really random? these duplicates look fishy

yes — each pack is sampled independently from mtgjson's sheet weights. real collation can balance colors and rarities within a pack, not between packs.

a typical sealed pool has ~10 duplicate pairs, almost all commons and uncommons. rare/mythic dupes feel surprising because they're memorable, but they come from the same random process. from 500 simulated bloomburrow pools:

  • ~15% of pools have a duplicate rare
  • ~1% have a duplicate mythic
  • ~1.6% have both a duplicate rare and a duplicate mythic

so an unusually dupe-heavy pool happens roughly every 60 sealed.
